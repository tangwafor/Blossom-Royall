## Delly's sixty second explanation

Blossom Royall is a shared marketplace for independent brands. Every vendor has a distinct presentation and keeps ownership of its products. Customers can discover several brands, build a complete look, and pay once at a shared cashier or online.

The system recognizes the vendor behind every item, updates the correct inventory, coordinates pickup or delivery, handles returns under the saved policy, and calculates the vendor's balance. Vendors can follow sales and stock remotely and receive payouts on the agreed schedule. That means a brand can maintain a real retail presence without keeping its own employee onsite every day.

The goal is not to hide small brands inside one generic store. The goal is to give each brand a stronger stage while sharing the expensive operational work.

## Questions to ask each vendor

1. Which products should customers discover first?
2. Which items can remain onsite, and which can be fulfilled from your own stock?
3. What sizes, colors, materials, and care information must be recorded?
4. What is your preferred return, alteration, preorder, and layaway policy?
5. How quickly can you replenish or fulfill an online order?
6. What payout schedule works for your business?
7. What brand story, provenance, or maker information should customers see?
8. Which events, appointments, fittings, or styling services could you offer?
9. What would make you feel fully confident placing inventory in the marketplace?

## Important language

Say that the projection calculator is illustrative and is not a revenue guarantee.
